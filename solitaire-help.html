How to play this solitaire implementation

  • Click the top card on the deck to deal to the kitty
  • Generally, click a visible card to select it to move
  • Once a card is selected, click where you want to move it
  • You can only click the rightmost card of the kitty
  • You can select any visible card on the board to move it and the cards on top of it
  • You can only move one card at a time to an upSuit column
  • You may move cards from an upSuit column to the board

How to play solitaire

  • The goal is to stack cards of each suit in order from ace to king in the four upSuit columns.
  • The board consists of the seven vertical columns; at the start of the game, each column has one face-up card.
  • Face-up cards on the board may be placed one atop the other, but the value must be decreasing from king through ace, and the suits must alternate colours, red-black and black to red, as a 3 of clubs may go on a 4 of diamonds, but a six of diamonds may not be placed upon a seven of hearts.
  • The game is a series of turns; if at the end, all cards are stacked correctly in the upSuit columns, you win.
  • In each turn, you can do one of the following:
    • You can deal from the deck to the kitty
    • You can redeal to the deck once its empty, if there are cards in the kitty
    • You can move the rightmost card of the kitty to either an upSuit column or a board column
    • You can move the topmost face-up cards of a board column to an upSuit column
    • You can move one or more face-up cards, in order, from one board column to another, provided they follow the decreasing value and alternating suit colour rules.
